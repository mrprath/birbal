> Thesis: Enterprise agent marketplaces are building the commercial and technical infrastructure most likely to produce a consumer-facing "App Store for AI agents" by 2027-2029, following the same enterprise-to-consumer pattern seen in every major platform shift from mainframes to mobile.

Today's enterprise agent marketplaces, AWS Marketplace's new AI Agents category, Salesforce's AgentExchange, Microsoft's Copilot Studio ecosystem, are laying the commercial and technical infrastructure most likely to produce a consumer-facing "App Store for AI agents." The pattern is not speculative; it has repeated in every major platform shift from mainframes to mobile. The question is not *if* this happens but *when*, and the current signals point to sooner than most expect.

## The Current Landscape: Enterprise Agent Marketplaces Are Real and Growing Fast

Two years ago, the phrase "agent marketplace" would have drawn blank stares outside of AI research circles. Today, every major cloud and enterprise platform either operates one or has announced plans.

AWS Marketplace launched a dedicated "AI Agents and Tools" category reportedly in mid-2025, announced alongside a $100 million investment in agentic AI development. The listings include SentinelOne's Purple AI security analyst, CrowdStrike's Falcon agentic extensions, and dozens of specialized tools. Critically, AWS added support for Model Context Protocol (MCP) and agent-to-agent (A2A) communication standards, meaning buyers can now filter agents by interoperability, not just function.

Salesforce launched AgentExchange in March 2025, explicitly calling it "the world's first agent marketplace." It launched with over 200 partners, Google Cloud, DocuSign, Box, selling pre-built Agentforce components. Partners build and list agentic components; buyers browse, evaluate, and deploy them inside their existing Salesforce environment.

Microsoft is building its agent marketplace more quietly but no less deliberately. Copilot Studio allows enterprises to build, customize, and publish agents internally. At Ignite 2025, Microsoft signaled broader marketplace ambitions with agent templates for specific verticals.

OpenAI's GPT Store is the closest thing to a consumer agent marketplace that currently exists. Over 3 million custom GPTs have been created, though only about 159,000 are publicly listed. The telling detail: as of early 2026, OpenAI still hasn't fully shipped its promised creator revenue-sharing program. It's an app store with apps but without an economy.

## The Historical Pattern: Enterprise Always Builds the Road That Consumer Traffic Eventually Uses

This is not the first time we've watched enterprise technology predict a consumer equivalent. The pattern is so consistent it barely qualifies as prediction.

- **Mainframes -> Personal Computers.** IBM built computing for enterprises in the 1960s. The technical primitives took 15-20 years to shrink to consumer form.
- **Enterprise Email -> Consumer Email.** Corporate email systems existed for a decade before Hotmail launched free email for consumers in 1996.
- **AWS -> DigitalOcean -> Vercel.** Amazon Web Services launched in 2006 selling raw cloud infrastructure. Each step was a compression of the same underlying technology into a more accessible form.
- **BlackBerry Enterprise -> iPhone Consumer.** BlackBerry dominated enterprise mobile from 2003-2007. The iPhone didn't beat BlackBerry by being a better enterprise tool. It beat BlackBerry by taking the *concept* and making it desirable to everyone.

The lag time between enterprise adoption and consumer equivalent has been compressing with each cycle: 15-20 years for mainframes-to-PCs, 10 years for enterprise email-to-Hotmail, 5-8 years for AWS-to-Vercel, 2-3 years for enterprise SaaS patterns reaching consumer SaaS. By this compression curve, the enterprise-to-consumer gap for AI agent marketplaces should be 2-4 years, meaning a genuine consumer agent marketplace between 2027 and 2029.

## What the Consumer Agent App Store Actually Looks Like

You open Claude (or ChatGPT, or whatever wins the interface war). Inside the app, there's a tab: "Agents" or "Store." You browse categories: Finance, Health, Productivity, Creative. Each listing has a name, ratings, a price, and a one-click "Add" button.

You tap "Add" on a tax preparation agent. It asks for permission to access your bank transactions. It files your taxes. You used to pay $200 for TurboTax or $400 for an accountant. This costs $29.

Who builds this platform? Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google: the companies that already own the AI interface layer. Who sells on it? Large software companies, vertical specialists, and solopreneurs who build niche agents the way indie developers build niche apps.

## Timeline and Signals: What to Watch

A consumer agent marketplace at scale requires four prerequisites, all currently in motion:

1. **Reliable agent execution.** Agents need to complete multi-step tasks with low failure rates. By late 2027, agent reliability for structured tasks should cross the trust threshold.
2. **Standardized interoperability.** MCP is becoming the de facto protocol. AWS Marketplace already labels listings by MCP compatibility.
3. **Identity and payment infrastructure.** The enterprise marketplaces are building exactly these layers right now.
4. **Consumer demand.** ChatGPT has 700 million users. The user base exists.

Realistic timeline: early consumer agent marketplaces by late 2027. A mature, large-scale marketplace by 2028-2029. The signal to watch: when Anthropic or OpenAI announces a creator revenue-sharing program with actual payment terms.

## What This Means for SMBs

A consumer agent marketplace is a leveling event of the kind that hasn't happened since cloud computing. A 5-person company with 10 well-chosen agents has the operational capacity of a 15-person company. That's not a productivity gain. That's a structural change in what "company size" means.

## Conclusion: The Rails Are Being Laid

Enterprise agent marketplaces are not a separate phenomenon from a future consumer agent store. They are the first phase of the same phenomenon. The consumer App Store for AI agents is not a moonshot prediction. It's an observation about a pattern that has repeated, without exception, in every major technology platform shift of the last 50 years. Enterprise builds the plumbing. Consumer makes it beautiful. The plumbing is being built right now.
